# HomeLink

**A personal path to a first home for young Albertans.**

HomeLink is a mobile app for Albertans aged 20–35 who are starting to earn and want to buy their first home. It turns "I'll never afford a home" into a personal plan with a target date: when you could buy, what price range fits, which first-time buyer programs you qualify for, and what to do next.

## Why

Homes in Alberta are more affordable than in much of Canada, yet first-time buyers keep getting older. What stops them usually isn't the price. It's saving a down payment while paying rent, and making sense of the mortgage rules and programs.

## What it does

- **Personal roadmap:** a target purchase date and price range based on your real income, savings and costs
- **Program finder:** the first-time buyer programs you qualify for, and how many months each one saves
- **What-if scenarios:** try a different city or home type, a higher savings rate, a partner's income or a rental suite
- **Progress tracking:** monthly check-ins, milestones and reminders
- **Short lessons:** 3–5 minute explainers tied to each step

HomeLink gives guidance, not financial advice. It launches in Calgary and Edmonton.

## Status

HomeLink is a free, personal demo project that I'm designing and building on my own. It's in the planning stage: the vision, first-release scope, personas and user stories are done, and requirements and design come next.
